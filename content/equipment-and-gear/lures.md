For those that don't want to get their hands dirty from [[baits|live bait]], lures are one of the best baits used to catch fish. Lures can be known as 'fake bait' and they can come in so many different combinations of sizes, patterns, and colors. A good amount of anglers enjoy collecting them and can not get enough of them. 

>The [[hooks]] on lures vary and they are often determined by the size of the lure.

## Common lures

### Jigs

They usually have a weighted metal-like head with a body and tail made of feathers, rubber skirts, soft plastics, or sometimes animal hair.

pic

### Soft plastics

These soft plastics can be made into lizards, worms, minnows, many other small creatures that are used to represent what fish typically eat. These plastics are known to come in different colors and sizes.

pic

### Spinnerbaits

Spinnerbaits typically have tails and bodies made of feathers, soft plastics, or animal hair that is connected to a 'safety pin' type shaft and there are metal pieces/blades on the other part of the safety pin that spin around.
