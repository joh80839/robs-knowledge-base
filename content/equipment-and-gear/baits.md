# What bait should you use?

Well, there are a variety of baits. You have live bait, plastic bait, jigs, spinnerbait, and many more. There are many fish that are opportunistic and will fish on a lot of different baits and there are fish that specialize in one type of food source. Freshwater fish have their own freshwater bait and it is the same for saltwater. For this page, we will focus on common live bait for freshwater and saltwater fish.

# Common Freshwater bait

### Worms (highly recommended)

The majority of opportunistic freshwater fish, which a good number of fish are, love worms. Worms such as red wrigglers, mealworms, earthworms found in the ground, or nightcrawlers found from bait shops are all good choices.

Cutting the worms into smaller pieces works very well for fish with small mouths, particularly for sunfish, such as bluegill.

### Crickets and grasshoppers

This bait can be small, but they are very appealing to crappie and sunfish, such as bluegill. Some bait shops sell them, but laying a cloth outside can attract crickets that you can use to catch fish with.

# Common Saltwater baits

### Shrimp (highly recommended)

This is one of the best baits for both freshwater and saltwater fish. Both dead or alive shrimp can work, and it’s known to catch speckled trout, redfish, and many more.

### Crab

This isn’t the cheapest bait to buy from stores but lots of saltwater fish will go after crab, whether it’s a whole crab or pieces of it.
