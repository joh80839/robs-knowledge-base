There are many types and brands of hooks, and they can all be useful, but the size of a hook is a huge factor, and it can determine what size fish and often the type of fish you are more likely to catch. Small hooks will allow you to catch small fish and big hooks will allow you to catch big fish. Texas has big fish, but it is has a lot of small-mouth fish and small fish in general, so having different size hooks increases your chances of catching fish in an area.

>The lower the number for a hook, the bigger the hook;
>the higher the number for a hook, the smaller the hook.

## Hook sizes

- Size 1-5 hooks will catch very big fish. Fish that can grow really big, such as catfish, trout, and largemouth bass, can be caught by these hooks. A small shark can even be caught on a hook like this.
- Size 5-10 hooks will catch medium to somewhat big fish, such as a fish around 8-10 inches, which can be bass and catfish.
- Size 10-18 hooks will catch small fish, typically bluegill, pumpkinseed sunfish, and potentially baby catfish.
- Size 18 and below can get into micro hook territory and this will catch minnows and small baby fish. 

>You can put live/natural [[baits]] on these hooks or they can be on [[lures]].
